01 // About This Research

Why Regional Trends Diverge

Global climate averages conceal sharp regional contrasts: while one sector warms or dries rapidly, another experiences shifting monsoon dynamics or wind regimes. We analyze 85-month NASA time series (2018–2025) across five distinct climatic zones.

02 // Who We Are

Aerospace & Earth Science Team

A student research team focused on aerospace engineering, atmospheric physics, and applied mathematical modeling—bridging satellite remote sensing with intuitive, transparent scientific visualization.

03 // What We Do

Statistical Detection & 3D Mapping

We process NASA Giovanni datasets (MERRA-2, GLDAS Noah, and Aqua AIRS), compute linear regression slopes and Mann-Kendall significance tests, and project regional findings onto an interactive 3D Earth model.
